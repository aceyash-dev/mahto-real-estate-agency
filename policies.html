Brokerage & Commission Policy

Mahto Real Estate Agency

1. Purpose

This page explains our general brokerage arrangements for property transactions facilitated by Mahto Real Estate Agency.

2. Standard Seller Brokerage

Where Mahto Real Estate Agency facilitates a property sale under an agreed seller-brokerage arrangement, the standard proposed commission is 2% of the agreed sale price, payable by the seller.

The written brokerage agreement must confirm the applicable calculation basis, payment responsibility, transaction milestone and any specific exceptions.

3. Transactions Involving Another Broker or Agent

Where another real estate broker or agent collaborates with Mahto Real Estate Agency, a separate commission arrangement may apply.

The proposed arrangement for qualifying collaborative transactions is a 1% commission payable by the buyer, subject to prior written agreement. The agreement must clearly state whether this is additional to, or instead of, any seller-side commission and who is responsible for each payment.

No customer should be charged an undisclosed commission.

4. When Commission Becomes Payable

The payment milestone must be specified in the written brokerage agreement. It may be linked to an agreed transaction event, subject to applicable law and the parties’ contract.

An enquiry or property visit alone does not automatically establish a commission obligation unless a valid agreement provides otherwise.

5. Other Costs

Stamp duty, registration charges, government fees, taxes, legal fees and third-party expenses are separate from brokerage unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing.

6. Receipts and Records

All commission payments should be documented with appropriate receipts and accounting records. Any invoice or tax treatment must follow applicable requirements.

7. No Hidden Charges

The agency will communicate the agreed commission terms before the customer commits to the transaction. Any variation should be recorded in writing and accepted by the relevant parties.

8. Disputes

Questions about a commission should first be raised through the agency’s support contact. This does not restrict any legal rights or remedies available under applicable law.

Property Verification & Disclaimer

Mahto Real Estate Agency

1. Our Role

Mahto Real Estate Agency assists customers with property enquiries, buyer-seller communication, property visits and transaction coordination within the agreed scope of service.

Our involvement as a broker does not automatically mean that we own the property, are its legal representative or have independently verified every document.

2. Property Information

Property details may be supplied by the owner, seller, authorized representative, builder or another broker. We will make reasonable efforts to publish accurate information and correct material errors when identified.

Customers should confirm important information before making a payment or entering into an agreement.

3. Verification Before Purchase

Buyers should seek appropriate independent verification of:

  • Ownership and title documents.
  • Jamabandi, khatiyan and other relevant land records.
  • Encumbrances, disputes and transfer restrictions.
  • Plot boundaries, area and access.
  • Land-use classification and permitted use.
  • Applicable layout approvals and development permissions.
  • Applicable RERA registration and project information.
  • Taxes, dues and other material liabilities.

The exact checks required depend on the property and transaction. A qualified property lawyer and relevant authorities should be consulted where appropriate.

4. Verification Status

A property may be labelled “Documents Received”, “Preliminary Review Completed” or “Independent Verification Completed” only when the description accurately reflects the work performed.

Receiving a document does not itself establish its authenticity or legal validity. No listing should be marked “100% legally verified” without a reliable basis for that claim.

5. RERA and Project Listings

Where RERA registration or other statutory approval is required, the agency must verify the applicable requirements before advertising or facilitating the transaction.

Relevant registration details should be displayed accurately. The agency must not facilitate transactions in projects where doing so would violate applicable RERA requirements.

6. Photographs and Maps

Images, boundaries and map locations are illustrative unless their accuracy has been established. Customers should inspect the actual property and confirm its boundaries before proceeding.

7. No Guarantee of Transaction Outcome

A listing or property visit does not guarantee availability, title clearance, loan approval, investment returns or completion of a transaction.

Nothing in this disclaimer removes any legal obligation or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
