Property Verification Disclaimer

Listings, if shown, are published only after an agency review process. That review is not a certification of ownership, title, boundaries, measurements, approvals, land use, encumbrances, litigation status, possession, valuation or suitability. Before paying money or signing, buyers should independently verify title and ownership chain, encumbrance and litigation records, land-use and planning permissions, layout/building approvals where applicable, tax and dues, actual measurements, access, possession, and the identity and authority of the person selling.
