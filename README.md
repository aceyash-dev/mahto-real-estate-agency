# Mahto Real Estate Agency

A mobile-first real estate website focused on plots and land, while supporting houses, flats, commercial properties, and other property types. The platform is intended to connect both customers and brokers for buying and selling, with clear enquiries and transparent agreed brokerage terms.

## Product principles

- Show only genuine, currently active listings approved by an administrator.
- Never fabricate properties, prices, reviews, availability, registration details, or verification claims.
- Distinguish customer and broker journeys while allowing both groups to enquire about buying and selling.
- Brokers must only submit listings they own or are authorized to market; moderation and reporting workflows should be planned from the start.
- Explain brokerage terms clearly and obtain agreement before services that incur a fee.
- Encourage independent property, title, approval, and document checks by qualified professionals. A listing or administrative review must never be presented as a legal guarantee.
- Collect only necessary enquiry data, disclose its use, and protect it with server-side validation, rate limiting, and spam controls.
- Follow applicable Indian and state-specific real estate requirements, including RERA obligations where applicable. Obtain professional legal review of the final policies and operating model.

## Planned journeys

### Customers
- Search properties by locality/district, type, budget, and area.
- View active approved listings and request more information or a visit.
- Submit a requirement to buy or sell a property.
- Contact the agency by phone, WhatsApp, or email.

### Brokers
- Register interest as a broker and provide contact/business details.
- Submit buy/sell requirements and propose property listings.
- Track enquiry status and communicate through approved contact channels.
- Listings remain unpublished until reviewed and approved by an administrator.

## Initial feature roadmap

- Responsive, accessible navigation and click-to-call / WhatsApp links.
- Property search and reset filters.
- Admin-controlled listing approval and active/inactive status.
- Customer and broker pathways with buy/sell intent.
- Secure enquiry forms with server-side validation, rate limiting, consent disclosure, and spam protection.
- Clear enquiry status and lead management for authorized staff (backend implementation to be scoped).
- Genuine image uploads or licensed property photography; no invented listings or representative images presented as actual properties.
- SEO metadata, canonical URLs, robots directives, sitemap, and structured data only where factual and appropriate.
- Reporting and moderation for inaccurate, unauthorized, duplicate, or suspicious listings.
- Privacy-aware data retention and access controls.

## Build approach

The website will be developed page by page as directed by the owner. Research informs recommendations, but features will be added deliberately rather than bloating the first release. No framework, database, hosting provider, or authentication provider has been selected yet.
