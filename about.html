Who we are

M.

Helping people connect around real property needs.

Mahto Real Estate Agency provides property enquiry, brokerage and transaction-coordination services, with a primary focus on plots and land and assistance with other property types when available.

Our objective is to help buyers and sellers connect, understand the available property information, arrange visits and communicate about transaction terms.

What we do

  • Assist customers in finding suitable plots and land.
  • Help property owners connect with prospective buyers.
  • Coordinate property visits and enquiries.
  • Facilitate communication between buyers, sellers and collaborating brokers.
  • Explain agreed brokerage arrangements before a transaction proceeds.
  • Assist with documentation coordination within the scope agreed by the parties.

Our approach

We aim to communicate clearly, use genuine listing information, explain brokerage terms and encourage appropriate verification before customers commit to a property transaction.

Property availability, ownership, approvals and legal status should be independently confirmed to the extent appropriate for each transaction. We do not claim government affiliation, statutory approval or professional certification unless supported by valid documentation.
